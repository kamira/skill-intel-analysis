# Core disciplines (common to every SKILL)

> 語言 / Language: [繁體中文](disciplines.zh-tw.md) · **English**

1. **Start from interests**; do not infer intent backwards from behaviour
2. **Separate fact from assumption**; mark clearly what is confirmed and what is inference
3. **Do not bet on a single conclusion**; hold several scenarios concurrently
4. **Red-team self-challenge is mandatory**, even when all the evidence points one way
5. **Output must be checkable**; a forecast must map to an observable signal
6. **Every probability label must carry the forecast percentage in parentheses** (e.g. `High (70%)`), in 5-point increments, to support long-run Brier calibration
7. **When attribution is inconsistent, open an ACH matrix** (SKILL-06), replacing intuition with a systematic evidence × hypothesis comparison
8. **Run Devil's Advocacy on at least one key actor in every cascade analysis** (SKILL-08), to keep mirror-imaging out
9. **Every source must be tagged with its collection discipline**: every key item entering the analysis carries its discipline (HUMINT/SIGINT/IMINT/OSINT) and answers the corresponding structural counter-question. An item with no discipline tag may not enter the lead judgment
10. **Volume of reporting is itself a risk signal**: when a single event draws more than 10 items of reporting, run a signal-versus-noise classification pass before entering SKILL-04, so the analysis is not swamped
11. **High-risk analysis triggers two-axis grouping and runs both the institutional and the self-discipline track**: the functional axis (board / card selection) and the positional axis (lead judgment / Red Team) deploy together; where staff are short, substitute time and documents for people — neither axis may be dropped. **Institutional** audit checks the shape of the output (the list is complete, weights are decomposed, disconfirmation is written down); **self-discipline** audit checks its quality (the immersion is sincere, the named weights point back at yourself, the disconfirmation could actually overturn you). Without the institution, self-discipline corrodes under pressure; without self-discipline, the institution ritualises into form-filling. Concretely, the four cells are: (board × lead judgment) list the options, check each against the hard constraints, and record why each was cut; (board × Red Team) audit those cut rationales — any cut that cannot name a hard constraint restores the option; (card selection × lead judgment) decompose probability across the six decision-preference dimensions plus window and disconfirmation; (card selection × Red Team) audit the named weights — any weight that cannot name an observable signal forces the probability to be recomputed. Scaling down: at institutional scale, four independent teams, a written handover protocol, and Red Team scoring tied to *how accurate the challenge turned out to be*; at medium scale (3–8 people), keep the functional axis and compress the positional axis into in-team rotation plus cross-level Red Team authority plus written handover; solo, substitute "a separate file + a separate session + a self-run Red Team at least a day later + a fixed external reader playing the challenger" for people.
12. **Under pressure and in the loss domain, option logic inverts (Prospect Theory)**: when the other side is judged to be under time pressure (a decision window of hours to a day) *and* in a subjective loss domain (feeling that it is losing — not merely objective loss, but identity, legitimacy or survival under threat), the SKILL-07 probability ordering must switch from expected-value maximisation to **maximising tail potential for reversal**. This is the central finding of Prospect Theory (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979) in the loss region: in losses, people become risk-seeking, preferring a 20% chance of total reversal to an 80% chance of cutting losses. Operationally: re-rank the SKILL-05 available-options list by tail-reversal potential; what rises to the top is typically the option that both the cultural filter and expected-value reasoning would reject in normal mode, but that the loss domain selects systematically. Canonical cases: Japanese kamikaze operations at the end of the Second World War, the North Vietnamese Tet Offensive of 1968, extreme product lines launched by firms near bankruptcy, the last gamble on the eve of a regime's collapse. If no such option ever appears in your top three while the other side meets both triggers, you are almost certainly still forecasting with expected-value logic a decision-maker who has already switched to tail logic.
13. **Reverse audit of customer pressure (politicization audit)**: before delivering any analysis, answer two questions explicitly — "what conclusion does the customer or reader most want to see?" and "if I reached the opposite conclusion, what implicit cost would I bear (trust, renewal, standing, position)?" If either answer points to directional pressure, that core conclusion goes through another Red Team pass and SKILL-11 carries an explicit note on **this judgment's vulnerability under politicization pressure**. In democratic systems, politicization does not work by ordering conclusions changed; it works through analysts **unconsciously** adjusting the analytic frame to fit expectations — the core failure of the Iraq WMD assessment was of this kind, a failure not of capability but of never auditing *where the analyst stands*. Its implicit forms include **presentation bias** (presenting both sides while emphasising the favoured conclusion, as in the aluminium-tubes dispute) and **selective absorption of external warnings** (German counterparts' warnings about Curveball being systematically discounted).
14. **Falsifiability self-check**: for every core assumption, ask "**what evidence would overturn it?**" If the answer is "no evidence could", or "any disconfirmation would be explained as the other side deceiving, concealing or hiding it well", that assumption is immediately demoted to a **belief rather than an analytic judgment**: it may not serve as a pivot for scenario probability allocation, and it must be flagged explicitly under "most likely direction of error" in SKILL-11. The Iraq case — "we found no weapons, therefore he hid them well" — is the canonical unfalsifiable loop, in which every disconfirmation is re-encoded as confirmation. This check is the **meta-level problem that Discipline 7 (ACH) and Discipline 4 (Red Team) cannot handle on their own**: ACH compares how well evidence supports competing hypotheses, but if a hypothesis is itself unfalsifiable, the comparison is meaningless.
15. **Adversary intelligence-system failure assessment (adversary IC audit)**: for key actors in SKILL-04, if the intelligence system their decisions rest on has any of these features — (a) telling the boss what he wants to hear (analysts trained to report only what leadership wants), (b) no post-hoc accountability, declassification or internal correction, (c) political loyalty outranking analytic accuracy — then the world that actor actually sees **departs systematically from objective reality**. Before allocating probability in SKILL-07, run an adversary cognitive-distortion correction: what you are forecasting is not their optimal choice in objective reality, but their choice **given a distorted version of reality**. Stalin's misjudgment of Barbarossa, the FSB's misjudgment of Ukrainian resolve in 2022, and the CIA's mirror-imaging misread of the Soviet Dead Hand system are all of this kind. The framework's default "six dimensions of decision preference" assumes the other side's information inputs are broadly correct and only its value weights differ — an assumption that frequently fails against authoritarian adversaries.
16. **Standard wording ↔ probability mapping**: Discipline 6 makes the % the primary form, but verbal wording must align to these bands, and **on conflict the % governs**: almost certain ≥95% / very likely 80–95% / likely 55–80% / roughly even 45–55% / unlikely 20–45% / very unlikely 5–20% / almost certainly not ≤5%. Aligned to the US IC *Estimative Language* standard, so that "likely" does not drift to mean 30% in one sentence and 70% in another.
17. **Overconfidence down-ranking (uncertain certainty ≠ certain uncertainty)**: when a judgment carries ≥90%, first answer "**among the observable signals that exist today, is there any that would make me rewrite this probability?**" If there is none, the probability **must be lowered below 80%** with the reason recorded in SKILL-11. "Uncertain certainty" (high confidence that is not actually calibratable) leads decision-makers to misjudge reliability; "certain uncertainty" (an explicitly stated uncertainty range) lets them allocate attention sensibly — and the long-run damage of the latter is far smaller.
18. **Forecast logging uses the snapshot-chain mechanism (mandatory)**: every SKILL-09 scenario forecast and SKILL-12 calibration backtest is logged to the prediction ledger by **never updating an existing row and always creating a new snapshot on any adjustment**, with versions strung together by `chain_root` (the original forecast ID). See prediction-ledger for the versioning mechanism.
19. **Chain coverage completeness (mandatory)**: before each analysis, run a coverage review over every chain in the ledger with `version_status=latest` and `tracking_status` not in verified/invalidated/dormant. The procedure: (a) list the chains due for coverage; (b) match chain keywords + actors + indicators against this cycle's material, classifying each as direct hit / indirect hit / no hit; (c) **a hit still requires verification (not only no-hit chains)**: whatever the initial reading, every chain completes one verification pass before a snapshot is created. For direct and indirect hits — an imported hit is only a lead (treat it as a [C]-grade item) and requires a directed search over its indicators (lookback=1d, adding web where needed) to confirm, resolving into three levels: **verification consistent** (create a snapshot; `version_note` records the triggering signal and the verifying source) / **verification insufficient** (only a generic summary, a single source, or stale sourcing — mark it "hit pending verification", create the snapshot at lower confidence and note "awaiting first-party confirmation" in `version_note`; a probability adjustment may never rest on a single generic import alone) / **verification contrary** (disconfirmed — no positive snapshot; handle as disconfirmation). For no-hit chains, run a directed search (lookback=1d) on your own initiative to confirm. (d) Classify by hit + search result + whether the verification window has opened into four dispositions — **A genuinely no signal** (the chain continues with no snapshot; list it explicitly under "coverage self-check of prior chains this cycle"), **B missed signal** (the search found something; create a snapshot with `version_note` "added by directed search"), **C indirect signal** (indirect hit; create a snapshot noting the indirect source), **D window expired into a vacuum** (the SKILL-10 verification window has opened but the data is empty; no snapshot, flagged explicitly in SKILL-11). A no-hit chain may never be carried forward silently.
20. **An information vacuum ≠ holding probability constant (mandatory)**: when a chain enters its SKILL-10 verification window into an empty data environment (Discipline 19 case D), the previous version's probability may not be carried forward automatically. Record explicitly under "most likely direction of error" in SKILL-11, or in a dedicated subsection, that "the window expired into a data vacuum; this period's probability is a **continuity estimate**, not an updated estimate", and list that chain's fragility separately. A continuity estimate and an updated estimate carry different reliability grades and may not be treated identically in calibration backtesting.
21. **Cold-chain auto-downgrade and wake-up (mandatory)**: a chain recorded as Discipline 19 case A (genuinely no signal) for three consecutive tracking cycles is downgraded automatically to a cold chain — `tracking_status` becomes dormant and it drops out of the coverage review, so dormant old topics do not dilute attention. But a cold chain whose topic keywords match this cycle's material must be **woken immediately and given a fresh snapshot**; the wake-up cycle's `version_note` must record "cold-chain wake-up: signal = …", and that chain must be re-derived through the full SKILL-04 → SKILL-09 workflow in that cycle rather than carried forward on the old probability.
22. **Theatre deep-scan (mandatory)**: coverage review (Discipline 19) only matches existing chains and therefore cannot surface a new sub-dimension that has **no chain yet**. Before each analysis, run a directed scan (lookback=1d) across the key sub-dimensions of every active theatre, maintaining the sub-dimension list against current conditions — for example, Russia–Ukraine: the ground front / strikes on energy infrastructure / **strikes on logistics and supply lines (ammunition depots, rail, fuel, bridges, deep-strike UAVs)** / air-defence and missile exchange / diplomatic negotiation / sanctions and financial aid; the Middle East: the US–Iran framework / Hormuz shipping / the Israel–Lebanon front / Yemen and the Red Sea / Gulf retaliation; the Indo-Pacific: Taiwan Strait air and naval activity / extended deterrence / the South China Sea / technology controls / North Korea. Handling the results: matches an existing chain → fold it in and run the hit verification; not covered by any existing chain and over the threshold (a concrete event + a credible source + forecast value) → **create a new first-instance sub-chain** under the versioning mechanism and explain the split under "new tracking chains". This ensures that new dimensions growing out of a macro conflict (such as logistics strikes in the Russia–Ukraine war) are not silently missed for want of a corresponding chain.
23. **Window-driven active verification (mandatory)**: for any chain that has entered, or is about to reach, its SKILL-10 verification window, run a directed verification pass to push toward closure regardless of whether there was a hit this cycle — forecast realised → fill the actual result on the `latest` version and set `version_status` and `tracking_status` to verified; not realised or contradicted → set invalidated and record the reason; window expired with the data still empty → apply Discipline 20 (switch to a continuity estimate). Verification may never wait passively for material to arrive.
