# SKILL-18|Structural trends

> 語言 / Language: [繁體中文](skill-18-structural-trends.zh-tw.md) · **English**

## Overview
Identify and assess the long-run structural trends that outlast any single event — demographics,
the energy transition, defence-industrial capacity, alliance drift — as the background baseline
and constraint set for all event analysis.

## Framework position
**Extension module**|Provides long-run structural background to every SKILL, interacting most with SKILL-04 (actor analysis), SKILL-05 (constraint screening) and SKILL-09 (scenario forecasting)

## Core question
> Which long-run structural trends are changing the underlying conditions? How do they constrain
> or widen actors' options?

## Governing principle
> **Events are waves on the surface; structural trends are the current beneath.** Most analysis
> focuses on events — the waves — but what determines where events go is usually the long-run
> trend. Ignore structural trends and you will observe the right event and reach the wrong
> conclusion, because the underlying conditions are no longer what you assume.

## Procedure
**1. Identify the relevant long-run trends**: which structural forces bear on the current issue
**2. Assess direction and speed**: whether the trend is accelerating, slowing or reversing
**3. Assess the effect on the analysis**: how it changes actors' constraints, capabilities and options
**4. Mark the inflection signals**: what would indicate the trend is turning
**5. Fold it into the event analysis**: return the trend judgment to the relevant SKILL as a background constraint

## Classes of structural trend

| Class | Coverage | Typical time scale |
|-------|----------|--------------------|
| **Demographics** | Ageing, labour-force change, urbanisation, migration | 10–30 years |
| **Energy transition** | Fossil → renewables, electrification, nuclear revival | 5–20 years |
| **Defence-industrial capacity** | Weapons production capacity, munitions stockpile rebuilds, new system fielding | 3–10 years |
| **Alliance systems** | Alliance cohesion, new alignments, non-alignment | 5–15 years |
| **Technological revolution** | Military AI, uncrewed systems, space, cyber | 3–10 years |
| **Economic centre of gravity** | Shifts in GDP share, supply-chain reconfiguration, evolution of the financial system | 5–20 years |
| **Institutional evolution** | Transformation of international order, changing function of multilateral bodies | 10–30 years |
| **Climate and resources** | Water stress, food security, frequency of extreme weather | 10–50 years |

## Cautions
- ⚠️ **A trend is not destiny** — policy, a technological breakthrough or a black swan can break it
- ⚠️ Distinguish a **trend** from a **fashion**: a genuine structural trend has an underlying driver and does not reverse on a short-term event
- ⚠️ Trends can reinforce or cancel one another — watch the interactions
- ✅ Review and update trend judgments on a regular cadence (monthly or quarterly)
- ✅ Log inflection signals in the SKILL-10 indicators
- 💡 The most valuable analytic insight often comes at the moment when **the direction of events contradicts the direction of the structural trend**
